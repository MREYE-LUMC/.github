We are the **MReye research group at Leiden University Medical Center (LUMC)**, a multidisciplinary team embedded within the **C.J. Gorter MRI Center** and the LUMC departments of **Ophthalmology, Radiology, and Radiation Oncology**.

We develop and evaluate new imaging, modelling, and computational techniques to improve the diagnosis, treatment, and understanding of ocular conditions. A major focus of our work is **ocular MRI and uveal melanoma**, alongside ocular proton therapy and patient-specific visual optics. Our goal is to translate technological innovation into better care for patients with eye disease.

## 🔬 Research

Our research brings together **medical imaging, physics, optics, radiotherapy, and ophthalmology**.
Research topics include:

- 🧲 [MRI for ocular oncology](https://mreye.nl/topics/ocular-oncology/)
- ☢️ [Ocular proton therapy](https://mreye.nl/topics/ocular-proton-therapy/)
- 👓 [Visual optics & patient-specific eye modelling](https://mreye.nl/topics/visual-optics/)

## 💻 Open science & software

We believe that research software and methods should be **reusable, transparent, and reproducible**. We therefore make our software, analysis methods, and — where privacy permits — research data openly available.

⭐ **Featured projects**:

- **[Visisipy](https://github.com/MREYE-LUMC/visisipy)**: a Python library for building eye models and performing clinically relevant optical simulations.
- **[pyROT](https://github.com/MREYE-LUMC/pyROT)**: a toolbox complementing RaySearch RayOcular for patient-specific ocular radiotherapy modelling.
- **[PAROS](https://github.com/MREYE-LUMC/PAROS)**: a method for determining and correcting the optical scaling of fundus photographs.

More projects can be found on our **[💻 software overview page](https://mreye.nl/open-science/software/)**.
